# Hercules

A mobile-first workout log: log a set in one or two taps, track PRs, and see your training on a calendar.

**Open it:** https://vpvishnu53.github.io/hercules/

On Android, open the link in Chrome and choose **Add to Home screen**. After the first visit it works offline. Your workouts are saved on your phone only. Use Settings → Export data to keep a backup.
